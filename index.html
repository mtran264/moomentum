      Your privacy

      Your data stays on your device. Your logs, activities, goals, and name are saved only in your browser's local storage on your device. We don't operate servers that receive this information, and we don't use cookies, analytics, or advertising.

      Hosting. Moomentum is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver the app's files, GitHub may process your IP address and standard request logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

      Fonts. The app's fonts are included with the app. Your browser doesn't contact any third-party font service.

      Install help. When you open Moomentum on a phone, the app checks your browser locally to show install steps. This check isn't saved or sent anywhere.

      Backups. Exporting creates a file that you save wherever you choose. It contains your logs and name, so keep it safe.

      Your control. You can view, change, export, or erase your data in the app at any time (Herd Management, Your data). Clearing your browser data or deleting the app also removes it. Data that's been erased cannot be recovered.
